5. DELIVERY


Sakz's Restaurant provides food delivery within selected areas
of Kathmandu.

Delivery hours:
11:00 AM - 9:30 PM

A standard delivery fee of NPR 100 applies to orders within the
regular delivery area.

Orders above NPR 2,000 receive free delivery within the regular
delivery area.

Delivery availability depends on the customer's location.

The restaurant does not currently provide delivery outside
Kathmandu.


6. RESERVATION POLICY


Customers can make reservations by phone or email.

Reservations are recommended for groups of 6 or more people.

A reservation should normally be made at least 2 hours before
the requested dining time.

The restaurant holds a reserved table for 15 minutes after the
reservation time.

For groups of 10 or more people, customers should contact the
restaurant in advance.

9. FAQ


Q: Does Sakz's Restaurant offer vegetarian food?

A: Yes. Sakz's Restaurant offers several vegetarian dishes,
including vegetable momo, vegetable chowmein, vegetable biryani,
paneer butter masala, vegetable pizza, and vegetable burger.

Q: Does the restaurant offer delivery?

A: Yes. Delivery is available within selected areas of Kathmandu.

Q: Can I reserve a table?

A: Yes. Reservations can be made by phone or email.

Q: Do you accept digital payments?

A: Yes. The restaurant accepts eSewa, Khalti, and bank QR payments,
as well as Visa and Mastercard.

Q: Is there a student discount?

A: Yes. Students receive 10% off selected food items from Monday
to Thursday between 11:00 AM and 5:00 PM with a valid student ID.

Q: What time does the kitchen close?

A: The kitchen closes 30 minutes before the restaurant's stated
closing time.

Q: Can I order delivery outside Kathmandu?

A: No. The restaurant currently provides delivery only within
selected areas of Kathmandu.
